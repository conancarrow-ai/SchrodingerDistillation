graph by k_v) that a SparseLinear neural network's fan_in parameter specifies K_G := max_{v in G} k_v where G is the graph of the network.
So, fan_in of some SparseLinear would be denoted by K in mathematical notation for the block diagram and other contexts. If what
I'm saying here is inaccurate, please write out the relationship between fan_in and K_G in some equations, and carry on so that the
